Heat Exchanger Duty & LMTD Calculator

Estimate exchanger duty, log mean temperature difference, required clean and fouled area, and whether the current service suggests thermal underperformance or fouling risk.

Scope of this version: single-pass screening tool based on Q = m·Cp·ΔT and LMTD-based area estimation. It is intended for fast engineering checks, not detailed mechanical design or rating. Real projects should still confirm exchanger type, correction factor, phase change, allowable pressure drop, and detailed fouling mechanisms.
